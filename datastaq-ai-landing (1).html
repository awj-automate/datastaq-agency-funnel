How is the quality actually "production-grade"?

+
Real architecture, real test coverage, real CI/CD, real monitoring. On the intro call I'll walk you through code from recent builds, the stack we use, and how we handle testing. You can also talk to current clients — I'd rather you hear it from them than from me.
